My Work as a Quality Assurance Analyst

  • Audit Content & Evaluate Moderation Accuracy: The core responsibility of a QA Analyst is sampling and reviewing content that has already been moderated (approved, rejected, escalated, or tagged) by front-line moderators.
  • Conduct Policy Calibration & Alignment Sessions: Content moderation policies often change rapidly due to emerging online trends, legal updates, or election cycles. QA Analysts lead alignment efforts across teams.
